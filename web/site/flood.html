Definition & detectors

Flash-flood risk (PROXY): ERA5 does not observe floods, so labels are reanalysis proxies. Default proxy_6h_api: 6-h total ≥ max(50 mm, local p99) and antecedent precipitation index > local p75 — too rare at ERA5 city points to train on, so detectors use the configured alternative p995_24h: 24-h total ≥ local p99.5 (DEVIATIONS.md).

Detectors: calibrated XGBoost at lead 6 h and 24 h vs a persistence rule. ~25 km cells cannot resolve convective cells or urban drainage — validate against NDMA/PDMA event records.
