Case study

Jacobabad, 2022 — one of the hottest cities on Earth, then the monsoon floods

Jacobabad (upper Sindh) recorded extreme pre-monsoon heat in 2022, and Sindh was among the provinces worst hit by the record monsoon rainfall from mid-June to late August. This page replays both windows of the held-out test year through the trained detectors: ERA5 observations, model probabilities, and the lead time of the first alert. Pick another city to compare.

Notes

Flash-flood labels are reanalysis proxies: ERA5 does not observe floods, and ~25 km cells cannot resolve convective cells or urban drainage.

Lead time of first alert = hours between the first predicted flag and the first labelled event in the window (negative = alert came after the event began).
