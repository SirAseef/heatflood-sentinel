Project

Scalable Big Data and Deep Learning Framework for Heatwave and Flash-Flood Forecasting and Detection in Pakistan — MS Data Science thesis, NED University of Engineering and Technology, Karachi. Student: Aseef Ahmed (DS-029/2025/26). Supervisor: Dr. Maria Andaleeb Siddiqui.

HeatFlood Sentinel ingests ERA5 reanalysis (and, once the data-sharing agreement is signed, PMD station records) through a medallion pipeline (bronze → silver → gold), forecasts 2 m temperature and precipitation with LSTM and ConvLSTM benchmarked against SARIMA and XGBoost, detects heatwaves and flash-flood risk, explains alerts with SHAP, and streams results through a Kafka / Spark Structured Streaming design (with a local fallback runtime) to this dashboard.

Hazard definitions (configs/hazards.yaml)

Heatwave: daily Tmax > local calendar-day 95th percentile (±7-day window, fitted on the training years) for ≥ 3 consecutive days. Alternative: Tmax ≥ 40 °C for ≥ 3 days.

Flash-flood risk (PROXY): default 6-h total ≥ max(50 mm, local p99) and antecedent precipitation index > local p75; alternative 24-h total ≥ local p99.5. ERA5 does not observe floods — these are reanalysis proxies.

Alert levels: Normal < 0.30 ≤ Advisory < 0.55 ≤ Watch < 0.75 ≤ Warning < 0.90 ≤ Emergency, with persistence/rain conditions, 2-step hysteresis and 3-h cooldown.

Limitations

  • Reanalysis is not observation; ERA5 (~25 km) cannot resolve convective cells or urban drainage.
  • Flood labels are proxies without hydrological routing; validate against NDMA/PDMA event records (data/external/events.csv).
  • dev-lite results (8 years, 20 cities, 2° grid) are indicative; the full profile (1991–2025, 0.25° grid) needs a workstation/cluster.
  • Streaming latencies were measured with the local fallback runtime on a 2-vCPU sandbox, not on a Kafka/Spark cluster.
  • PMD station data not yet available (agreement pending) — adapter implemented and tested on synthetic PMD-like files only.
  • Live page uses Open-Meteo's forecast model, not ERA5, for the most recent ~5 days and the 72-h outlook.
