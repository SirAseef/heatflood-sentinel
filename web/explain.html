Comparison with prior PMD heatwave work

Shafiq et al. (2025) explained a PMD-station heatwave model with SHAP and found temperature-persistence and humidity-related predictors dominant. Here, for heat detection the leading drivers are likewise the current daily maximum, its anomaly versus the calendar-day normal and the hot-day run length (persistence), qualitatively consistent; for flood-risk, recent 6-/24-h accumulations and antecedent wetness dominate. This is a qualitative comparison only — numbers are not comparable (different data, labels and models).
