Notes

(a) Pre-monsoon heat 2022: Pakistan and north-west India saw record heat from March to May 2022 (Jacobabad > 50 °C in May). (b) Monsoon 2022: record rainfall from mid-June to late August submerged large parts of Sindh and Balochistan. Flash-flood labels here are reanalysis proxies (ERA5 does not observe floods; ~25 km cells cannot resolve convective cells or urban drainage). (c) The April 2025 heat episode requires 2025 data (make data with a later year range) and is not part of this run. Lead time of first alert = hours between the first predicted flag and the first labelled event in the window.
