Definition & detectors

Heatwave: daily Tmax > the local calendar-day 95th percentile (±7-day window, fitted on the training years 2015–2020) for ≥ 3 consecutive days. Alternative definition: Tmax ≥ 40 °C for ≥ 3 days.

Detectors: calibrated XGBoost (isotonic on 2021, threshold maximising CSI subject to FAR ≤ 0.5) at lead 1 d and 3 d, a CNN on the 2° grid, a persistence rule (tomorrow = today) and an LSTM-forecast rule.
