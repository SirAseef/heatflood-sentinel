MS Data Science thesis · NED University of Engineering and Technology, Karachi

HeatFlood Sentinel

Scalable Big Data and Deep Learning Framework for Heatwave and Flash-Flood Forecasting and Detection in Pakistan

LSTM, ConvLSTM, XGBoost and SARIMA models trained on ERA5 reanalysis for 20 Pakistani cities, benchmarked on a held-out year, calibrated heatwave and flash-flood-risk detectors, a Hadoop/YARN · Spark · Kafka pipeline, and a live Open-Meteo feed pushed through a streaming alert engine every 6 hours.
