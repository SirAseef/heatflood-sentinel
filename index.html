MS Data Science thesis · NED University of Engineering and Technology, Karachi

Scalable Big Data and Deep Learning Framework for Heatwave and Flash-Flood Forecasting and Detection in Pakistan

HeatFlood Sentinel trains LSTM, ConvLSTM, XGBoost and SARIMA models on real ERA5 reanalysis for 20 Pakistani cities (2015–2022), benchmarks them honestly on the held-out year 2022, detects heatwaves and flash-flood risk with calibrated classifiers, and pushes a live Open-Meteo feed through a streaming alert engine. This site is the public, static edition of the project dashboard; its live snapshot is regenerated every 6 hours by GitHub Actions.
